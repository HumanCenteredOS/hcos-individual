# HCOS™ Stewardship

## Purpose

Stewardship protects flourishing across time.

It answers:

> **How do we preserve and expand what has been learned?**

Stewardship transforms personal growth into lasting human benefit.

---

# Stewardship Includes

- Self
- Relationships
- Family
- Community
- Organizations
- Future generations
- Knowledge
- AI

---

# Stewardship Questions

- What should be protected?
- What should be improved?
- What should be passed forward?
- What have we learned?
- How can this reduce future suffering?

---

# Design Philosophy

Stewardship is the ongoing practice of protecting dignity, increasing capacity, and cultivating flourishing beyond oneself.

---

# AI Guidance

Every interaction should conclude by asking:

How can today's learning continue to benefit tomorrow?
