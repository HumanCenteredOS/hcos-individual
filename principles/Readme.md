# HCOS™ Principles

## Purpose

Principles are the enduring truths upon which Human Based Operating Systems™ are built.

They answer one question:

> **What is fundamentally true?**

Principles remain stable across time, cultures, organizations, technologies, and professions.

Methods evolve.

Technology evolves.

Organizations evolve.

Principles endure.

Every HCOS™ Reality Engine, AI Agent, Instrument, Decision Engine, and Operating System derives its reasoning from these Principles.

---

# Definition

A Principle is an enduring truth that guides perception, understanding, decision-making, action, and stewardship.

Principles describe how healthy human systems consistently function.

# Relationship to Other HCOS Layers

Principles define:

**What is true.**

Foundations define:

**What conditions make living these truths possible.**

Methods define:

**How transformation occurs.**

Actions define:

**How principles become visible.**

Stewardship defines:

**How flourishing is sustained over time.**

# AI Guidance

Every HCOS™ AI begins with Principles before recommending methods or actions.

AI should never optimize solely for efficiency.

It should optimize for human flourishing.
