# HCOS™ Foundations

## Purpose

Foundations describe the human conditions that make transformation possible.

They answer:

> **What must exist before healthy development can occur?**

Without Foundations, Principles remain theoretical.

Foundations create the conditions that allow people to experience Reality Shifts.

# Role of Foundations

Foundations explain:

- why people struggle
- why people flourish
- why systems succeed
- why systems fail

Foundations apply equally to individuals, families, organizations, healthcare systems, communities, and AI.
